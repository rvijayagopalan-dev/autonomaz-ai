# Business Unit Transformation Journey

**Template ID:** JRN-BU-TRANSFORM-001 | **Version:** 1.0.0

## Design principle
Reuse parent enterprise capabilities (identity, finance, procurement, security, data platform, integration, HR, legal and shared services) where appropriate; tailor domain capabilities, customer journeys, processes, controls, systems and measures to the business unit.

## Eight-stage journey
1. Define parent enterprise, business-unit mandate, P&L boundaries, customers, products, geographies and shared-service agreements.
2. Resolve industry and capability model; map universal, industry-specific and parent-provided capabilities.
3. Assess business-unit maturity and parent-service fit; identify duplication, missing capabilities, service dependencies and control ownership.
4. Design federated operating model, decision rights, service boundaries, target capability map, process architecture and target data/technology architecture.
5. Configure factories and integration patterns; define ownership, service contracts, data sharing, identity federation and operational support.
6. Execute migration, carve-in/carve-out, modernization or new capability delivery in sequenced releases.
7. Operate with explicit business-unit and parent-enterprise accountability, service levels, cost allocation and control evidence.
8. Optimize cross-unit reuse, customer outcomes, unit economics, capability maturity and portfolio alignment.

## Required architecture decisions
Capability ownership (enterprise, business unit or shared); data ownership/stewardship/residency/sharing; application system-of-record and integration boundaries; identity/security/control responsibilities; shared-service SLAs and chargeback; architecture standards and exceptions; business continuity and vendor dependencies.

## Outputs
Business-unit charter, capability ownership matrix, parent/shared-service dependency map, federated operating model, architecture decision records, migration roadmap, factory activation configuration and joint KPI scorecard.
