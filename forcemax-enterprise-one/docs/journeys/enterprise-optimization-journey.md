# Enterprise Optimization Journey Template

**Template ID:** JRN-OPT-001 | **Version:** 1.0.0

## Purpose
Improve an existing enterprise or business unit using evidence-led diagnosis and measurable value realization.

## Entry criteria
- Executive sponsor and business owner identified.
- Current organization and operating scope known.
- Access to relevant process, technology, data, risk and financial evidence authorized.
- Optimization outcomes and constraints agreed.

## Eight-stage plan
1. **Discover:** capture strategy, business model, customers, products, organization, value streams, systems, suppliers, jurisdictions and current KPIs.
2. **Assess:** baseline capabilities, processes, workforce, data, applications, infrastructure, security, risk, cost and customer outcomes. Record evidence, assessor, confidence and date.
3. **Diagnose:** map gaps to capability IDs; identify root causes, process bottlenecks, duplicated applications, data quality issues, control weaknesses, technical debt, cost drivers and automation opportunities.
4. **Design:** define target-state options, target operating model, architecture, controls, transition state, dependencies, business case and measurable outcomes.
5. **Configure:** select factories and reusable accelerators; validate access, policies, budget, dependencies, environment and approvals.
6. **Execute:** sequence quick wins and structural changes; manage delivery, testing, adoption, cutover, rollback and operational handover.
7. **Operate:** track leading/lagging KPIs, SLOs, risk/control health, cost, incidents and customer experience.
8. **Optimize:** compare actuals to approved baseline/target; verify benefits; identify bottlenecks; refresh assessment and improvement backlog.

## Prioritization rubric
Strategic alignment (0–5), customer/business value (0–5), risk reduction/regulatory urgency (0–5), feasibility/readiness (0–5), time-to-value (0–5). Record effort and dependencies separately. Weighted score is decision support, not an automatic decision. Show assumptions, confidence, evidence, owner and rationale; require business approval.

## Deliverables
Current-state assessment, evidence register, capability heatmap, process diagnostics, application/data/technology inventory, risk/control findings, opportunity portfolio, target blueprint, roadmap, business case, factory activation plan, KPI scorecard and verified benefits register.

## Metrics
Capability maturity movement; process cycle time; first-time-right quality; cost-to-serve; application rationalization; incident rate; control exceptions; data quality; customer satisfaction; realized benefits versus approved baseline.
