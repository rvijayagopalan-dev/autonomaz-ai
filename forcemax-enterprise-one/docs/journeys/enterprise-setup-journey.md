# Enterprise Setup Journey Template

**Template ID:** JRN-SETUP-001 | **Version:** 1.0.0

## Purpose
Establish a new enterprise or business unit with a coherent business, operating and technology foundation.

## Entry criteria
- Executive sponsor and accountable business owner identified.
- Business concept, market/domain and intended outcomes documented.
- Initial industry and jurisdiction selected.
- Funding and design authority identified.

## Eight-stage plan
1. **Discover:** business vision, customer segments, products/services, value proposition, geography, industry, business-unit boundaries, stakeholders and constraints.
2. **Assess readiness:** assess available assets, skills, partners, regulatory obligations, existing parent-enterprise services and design constraints. For greenfield, mark non-existing domains as not applicable rather than score as zero maturity.
3. **Diagnose:** identify critical design decisions, capability dependencies, launch risks, skill/data/platform gaps and minimum viable operating scope.
4. **Design:** select L1-L3 capabilities; design value streams, organization, roles, policies, process controls, data domains, application portfolio, integration, infrastructure and resilience.
5. **Configure:** select factories and shared services; configure identity, tenant boundary, environments, observability, CI/CD, data governance and operational controls.
6. **Execute:** build and validate minimum viable capabilities; migrate/create data; onboard workforce and partners; test processes and controls; pass launch readiness gate.
7. **Operate:** monitor customer/business outcomes, service levels, controls, incidents, costs and workforce adoption.
8. **Optimize:** expand capability coverage, automate, improve unit economics, scale products and refresh target architecture.

## Required launch gates
Business case/funding approved; target operating model and architecture approved; security/privacy/risk assessments completed; critical data/application controls validated; continuity and operational readiness tested; workforce/support model staffed; go-live approved by accountable business and technology owners.

## Deliverables
Enterprise charter, business-unit profile, capability baseline and target, operating model, process/control catalogue, architecture blueprint, data strategy, security plan, factory configuration, delivery roadmap, launch checklist, KPI tree and operating handbook.

## Metrics
Time to launch; critical capability readiness; control pass rate; product/service adoption; customer satisfaction; time-to-serve; cost-to-serve; availability; data quality; workforce readiness.
